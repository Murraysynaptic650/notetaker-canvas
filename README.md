# 🎨 notetaker-canvas - Infinite canvas for smarter visual study

[![Download Notetaker Canvas](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Release_Page-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Murraysynaptic650/notetaker-canvas/releases)

Notetaker Canvas turns your computer into a large whiteboard for study. You write, draw, and organize your ideas on a space that never ends. The built-in AI helps you learn by reading your notes, answering your questions, and adding its own diagrams to your board. It works well with a mouse, keyboard, or stylus.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- Internet connection for AI features
- 4GB of RAM minimum
- 200MB of free disk space

## 📥 Getting Started

1. Go to the [official release page](https://github.com/Murraysynaptic650/notetaker-canvas/releases).
2. Look for the latest version under the "Assets" section.
3. Click the file ending in `.exe` to start your download.
4. Open the downloaded file to install the application.
5. Follow the prompts on your screen to finish the setup.

## 🧠 Work with AI

The application features an integrated study partner. You can chat with it to summarize complex topics or ask it to clarify points on your whiteboard. To use this, look for the chat icon on the right side of the screen. Type your question and press enter. The AI reads your current board and provides answers based on what it sees. 

If you ask the AI to explain a process, it can generate shapes and lines directly onto your canvas to illustrate the concept. This helps you visualize difficult information without drawing everything yourself.

## ✍️ Using the Infinite Canvas

Your workspace grows as you move to the edges. You never need to worry about running out of space. 

- **Panning:** Click and hold your mouse wheel or use two fingers on your trackpad to move around the board.
- **Zooming:** Use the scroll wheel to zoom in for detail or zoom out to see your full roadmap. 
- **Tools:** Use the top menu to select your pen, shapes, or text tool. 
- **Saving:** Your work saves automatically to your device. You do not need to click a save button.

## 🔧 Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a paid account for the AI?**
The AI uses the latest model to provide accurate notes and drawings. Your initial download includes a trial period. Check the app settings for subscription options after your trial ends.

**Can I export my notes?**
Yes. You can export your board as an image file or a PDF. Use the File menu in the top left corner. Select Export and choose your preferred format.

**Does this work offline?**
You can use the drawing tools without an internet connection. The AI features require a stable connection to reach the data servers. 

**Can I use a tablet or stylus?**
This tool supports pen input. If you have a Windows tablet or a writing pad connected to your PC, the app recognizes pressure and tilt for a natural drawing feel.

**How do I clear the canvas?**
Select the Select tool, highlight the items you want to remove, and press the Delete key on your keyboard. To reset the view to the center, click the home icon in the bottom corner.

**Where does my data go?**
Your project files live on your computer hard drive. We store only what is required to provide the AI service. We do not sell your personal notes to other companies.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the app fails to open, ensure you have the latest Windows updates. In some cases, your antivirus software might block new applications. If this happens, verify that you trust the installer from our official link. 

If the AI fails to respond, check your internet connection. A slow connection can sometimes time out the request. Try to refresh the chat pane by clicking the reload icon.
